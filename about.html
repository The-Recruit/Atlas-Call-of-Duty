// OUR ORIGIN

BORN FROM NECESSITY

ATLAS Corporation was founded in 2035 by Jonathan Irons, a visionary whose belief in order, strength, and human potential transcended the limitations of traditional governance. Having witnessed firsthand how political bureaucracy and national self-interest crippled military effectiveness during the conflicts of the early 21st century, Irons set out to build an organization unconstrained by borders, politics, or public opinion.

Starting with a modest private security contract in West Africa, ATLAS rapidly expanded its operational scope and technical capabilities. Within a decade, the corporation had grown from a 500-man security firm into a multi-billion dollar conglomerate employing over 1,850,000 personnel across 49 nations.

ATLAS operates under a simple philosophy: where governments hesitate, we act. Where systems fail, we rebuild. Where order collapses, we restore it — with precision, with speed, and with absolute commitment to mission success.

Today, ATLAS Corporation stands at the intersection of military might and infrastructure excellence, offering a full spectrum of services from tier-one combat operations to post-conflict reconstruction at scale. We are not constrained by national interest; we serve global stability itself.

COMPANY HISTORY

2035

FOUNDING

Jonathan Irons establishes ATLAS Corporation in Geneva with initial capital of $4.2 billion and a 500-man special operations unit. First contract: security stabilization in West Africa.

2038

RAPID EXPANSION

ATLAS secures its first major government contract — a $40 billion infrastructure reconstruction deal in the Gulf region following regional conflict. Personnel numbers surpass 25,000.

2041

TECHNOLOGY DIVISION LAUNCH

ATLAS Advanced Systems division created, pioneering exoskeleton technology, autonomous weapons platforms, and advanced surveillance infrastructure for battlefield deployment.

2045

GLOBAL LEADER STATUS

ATLAS surpasses all national military contractors to become the world's largest private military and infrastructure company. Active operations in 35+ nations. Revenue exceeds $400 billion.

2050

NEW BAGHDAD CAMPUS

Completion of the Atlas New Baghdad Operational Complex — the world's largest combined military-corporate campus, housing 40,000 personnel and serving as global HQ.

2054

PRESENT DAY

ATLAS operates in 49 nations with 1,850,000+ active personnel. Revenue: $890 billion. The undisputed global leader in private military and infrastructure services.
